## Seeding

Teams are seeded by Valve Regional Standings (VRS), then the HLTV world ranking, then the average FACEIT Elo of the five starting players, then registration order. A team seeded by a lower criterion is never placed above a team seeded by a higher one. A team only inherits a VRS or HLTV ranking when its registered roster keeps at least three players from the roster that earned it.
